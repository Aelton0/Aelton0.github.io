CASE 02 / ANALYTICS ENGINEERING & STATISTICS

Revenue Risk Analysis

A statistical analysis rejecting distorted arithmetic averages in favor of median-based ABC curve clustering, uncovering an existential concentration vulnerability.

01 / THE CONTEXT & PROBLEM

The Statistical Trap of Arithmetic Averages

In growing companies, executive reporting commonly relies on arithmetic means (`AVG(revenue_per_client)`). When customer portfolios exhibit heavy positive skewness (Pareto distributions), an arithmetic mean is severely distorted by a handful of large contracts.

The board believed the customer base was distributed around a healthy mid-tier ticket of R$ 18,900. In reality, hundreds of small clients were subsidizing operational churn while an ultra-thin tier of 5 accounts held the entire cash flow solvency of the business.

02 / METHODOLOGY

Non-Parametric Order Statistics

Median-Based Segmentation

By utilizing the median (`PERCENTILE_CONT(0.50)`), the true central tendency was established at R$ 4,200 — exposing that 80% of accounts were transacting at less than a quarter of the reported "average".

Cumulative Concentration Curves

Accounts were dynamically ranked and evaluated on running total shares, establishing three discrete risk tiers: Tier A (Critical 70%), Tier B (Core 20%), and Tier C (Long tail 10%).

03 / SQL IMPLEMENTATION

Dimensional Query with Window Functions

WITH client_aggregates AS (
    SELECT 
        c.client_id,
        c.company_name,
        SUM(t.net_amount) AS total_revenue,
        COUNT(DISTINCT t.invoice_id) AS total_invoices,
        PERCENTILE_CONT(0.5) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY t.net_amount) AS median_invoice_amount
    FROM dim_clients c
    JOIN fact_invoices t ON c.client_id = t.client_id
    WHERE t.status = 'PAID'
      AND t.paid_at >= CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '12 months'
    GROUP BY c.client_id, c.company_name
),
cumulative_revenue AS (
    SELECT 
        client_id,
        company_name,
        total_revenue,
        median_invoice_amount,
        SUM(total_revenue) OVER (ORDER BY total_revenue DESC ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW) AS running_total,
        SUM(total_revenue) OVER () AS total_company_revenue
    FROM client_aggregates
)
SELECT 
    client_id,
    company_name,
    total_revenue,
    median_invoice_amount,
    ROUND((total_revenue / total_company_revenue) * 100, 2) AS revenue_share_pct,
    ROUND((running_total / total_company_revenue) * 100, 2) AS cumulative_share_pct,
    CASE 
        WHEN (running_total / total_company_revenue) <= 0.70 THEN 'Tier A (Critical Concentration)'
        WHEN (running_total / total_company_revenue) <= 0.90 THEN 'Tier B (Core Base)'
        ELSE 'Tier C (Long Tail)'
    END AS abc_tier
FROM cumulative_revenue
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;
04 / STRATEGIC IMPACT

Strategic Decision-Making

The presentation of this statistical model triggered immediate governance actions by startup leadership:

  • Executive Retention Taskforce: Immediate assignment of senior account leads to the top 5 clients representing 70% of gross volume.
  • Commercial Realignment: Redirection of outbound sales campaigns towards mid-market tiers (R$ 5,000–12,000/mo) to systematically reduce portfolio concentration risk over the subsequent 4 quarters.
